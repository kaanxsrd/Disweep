## Inspiration

Disweep was inspired by **Undiscord** by [Victornpb](https://github.com/victornpb) and its contributors, a browser userscript for deleting Discord messages:

<https://github.com/victornpb/undiscord>

Disweep is an independent program written from scratch and contains none of Undiscord's code. Thank you to its authors for showing what people need from a tool like this.
